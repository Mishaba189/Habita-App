# Habita

**Habita App** is a personal habit tracker designed to help you build great routines, break bad habits, and achieve your daily goals with style and consistency[cite: 1].

---

## 📲 Download the App (Android)

You can download the latest official APK directly from the **Releases** page:

👉 **[Download Latest APK (v1.0.0)](https://github.com/Mishaba189/Habita-App/releases/latest)**

### How to Install
1. Tap the download link above on your Android device to get the `.apk` file.
2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or **Downloads** folder.
3. If prompted by Android, enable **Install from Unknown Sources** (or *Allow from this source*) for your browser/file manager.
4. Tap **Install** and launch **Habita**.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Goal-Oriented Habit Tracking:** Pair daily habits with larger milestone goals [cite: 1].
* **Flexible Scheduling:** Choose schedules that fit your routine—**Everyday**, **Weekdays (Mon–Fri)**, **Weekends Only**, **Specific Days**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**[cite: 1].
* **Custom Target Periods:** Set structured timeframes such as **1 Week (7 Days)**, **2 Weeks (14 Days)**, or **1 Month (30 Days)**[cite: 1].
* **Visual Progress & Insights:** Track daily completion percentages, monitor overall goal achievement rings, and keep streaks alive with a clean, distraction-free interface[cite: 1].
* **Smart Reminders & Privacy:** Stay on track with push notifications while keeping your personal data secure[cite: 1].
